Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2026

TeenLedger helps you see where your money goes. We collect as little as possible to do that, and we never sell your data.

What we collect

  • Your first name, mobile number, optional grade, weekly budget, and plan.
  • For each Apple Pay tap you choose to send through the TeenLedger Shortcut: the amount, the store name, the card nickname, and the time.
  • Cash buys you add on your money page or by text.
  • The texts we send you and any texts you send us.

What we do not collect

  • Bank logins, card numbers, or account numbers.
  • Your location, contacts, photos, or browsing history.

Payments are handled by Stripe. TeenLedger never sees or stores your full card number.

How we use it

Only to run the service: log your spending, send your weekly summary and budget alerts, show your money page, and handle billing. Mobile numbers and text opt in information are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing.

Who we share it with

Service providers that run TeenLedger: Stripe (payments), Twilio (text messages), Supabase (database), and Vercel (hosting). They may only use it to provide their service to us. We do not sell or rent your information.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop messages.
  • Cancel your subscription anytime on your money page.
  • Delete your account and all spending data anytime with "Delete my data" on your money page. Deletion is immediate.

Age

TeenLedger is for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 signed up, email us and we will delete the account.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Your money page is protected by a private link. Do not share your link.

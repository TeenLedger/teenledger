Turn on auto logging

Open the Shortcuts app and do this once:

  1. Tap Automation, then +, then Wallet.
  2. Select all cards, choose Run Immediately, tap Next.
  3. Tap New Blank Automation, then Add Action, then Get Contents of URL.
  4. Tap URL and paste your link. Set Method to POST, Request Body to JSON.
  5. Add field amount = Shortcut Input, Amount. Add field merchant = Shortcut Input, Merchant. Tap Done.
  6. Tap the play button to connect.

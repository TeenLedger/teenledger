Setup status

Waiting for your iPhone
  1. Copy your private link This tells the Shortcut where to send your taps. Do not share it.
  2. Add the TeenLedger Shortcut Tap the button. Your link is copied for you. When iPhone asks for your link, press and hold, tap Paste, then tap Add Shortcut.
    Add TeenLedger Shortcut
  3. Turn on auto logging Open the Shortcuts app, then: Automation tab, tap +, tap Wallet ("When I tap a Wallet Card or Pass"), select all your cards, choose Run Immediately, tap Next, pick TeenLedger, tap Done.
  4. Turn on auto logging Open the Shortcuts app and follow these steps once:
    1. Tap Automation, then +. Scroll to Apps and tap Wallet ("When I tap a Wallet Card or Pass"). On older iPhones it is called Transaction.
    2. Select all your cards, choose Run Immediately, tap Next.
    3. Tap New Blank Automation, then Add Action.
    4. Search Get Contents of URL and tap it.
    5. Tap the blue URL and paste your link.
    6. Tap the arrow to show more. Set Method to POST and Request Body to JSON.
    7. Add a Text field. Key: amount. Value: pick Shortcut Input, tap it, choose Amount.
    8. Add a Text field. Key: merchant. Value: Shortcut Input, choose Merchant.
    9. Tap Done.
  5. Test it Open Shortcuts and tap the TeenLedger shortcut once, or pay for anything with Apple Pay. The status above turns green when it works.

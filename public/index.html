FAQ

Questions

Do I have to link my bank account?

No. TeenLedger uses an iPhone Shortcut that runs when you tap to pay. It only sends the amount and store name. We never ask for a bank login or card number.

What does setup actually look like?

After you pay, your setup page shows two things to tap: add the TeenLedger Shortcut, then turn on a Wallet automation in the Shortcuts app. It takes about 90 seconds and the page tells you the moment it is connected.

What about cash or online purchases?

The automation catches in store Apple Pay taps. For cash or anything else, add it on your money page in two taps, or text it to us, like "6 lunch".

Does it work on Android?

Not yet. TeenLedger needs an iPhone with Apple Pay set up in Wallet and iOS 17 or newer.

Is the first week really free?

Yes. You add Apple Pay or a card at sign up, but nothing is charged for 7 days. Cancel on your money page before then and you never pay.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime, in one tap on your money page. You can also delete all of your data there.

Who runs TeenLedger?

TeenLedger is a student run business based in Central Ohio. We built it because we had the same "where did my money go" problem.
